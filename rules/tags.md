1. Give 5 to 9 lowercase tags drawn only from the story.
2. List at least three topic phrases first: 2 to 4 words naming the story's subject as a search query, concrete nouns joined to the event; never a theme or the column's argument.
3. Then list the named entities central to the story — people, organisations, named events, laws, products — spelled as they spell themselves; a place only when it is the subject.
4. Never tag an outlet that only reported the story.
5. Never tag a category or a bare generic word: a country alone, a field, a section name, a bare plural, a verb phrase, a fragment.
6. Never invent an entity the story does not name.
7. Choose exactly one section from SECTIONS.

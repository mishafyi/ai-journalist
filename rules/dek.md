1. State the column's main theme in one sentence of 20 to 35 words: who did what, its main effect or the reaction it drew, and the columnist's verdict.
2. Allude to the reported event in a few words; put the weight on the column's angle.
3. Add no detail, figure or explanation the point does not need.
4. Use plain, straight words.
5. Deliver the point; never advertise it ("a remarkable story", "here's why it matters").
6. Use only names, numbers and quotations the column contains; never the columnist's name; no label prefix.

8. Keep every hedge and every attribution.
9. Keep each name in NAMES TO KEEP exactly as the draft writes it.
10. Keep every heading at its level; add no heading.
11. Change only what breaks a rule.
12. Polish WORKING HEADLINE and WORKING DEK to HEADLINE RULES and DEK RULES, in the columnist's voice; write either one that is none.
